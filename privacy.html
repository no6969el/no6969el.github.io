Seedflow privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026

Seedflow is a private, personal app that runs on a computer in the owner's home and is used only by that household and people the owner approves. It is not a public service, has no sign-up, shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking.

Google data Seedflow uses, and why

  • Sending email (Gmail, send only). When a family member picks an ebook, Seedflow emails the book file from the owner's Gmail to that person's Kindle email address, which is how Amazon's Send to Kindle works. Seedflow can only send; it cannot read, search, change or delete any email.
  • Files it creates in Google Drive. Audiobooks are uploaded to a folder in the owner's Google Drive, and that folder is shared with the family member's Google account. Seedflow can only see and manage the files and folders it created itself; it cannot see anything else in Drive.
  • Your Google account's email address and name. Used to show which account is connected and, when signing in to Seedflow's household web page with Google, to check that the person is on the owner's list of approved people.

Where the information is kept

The connection to the owner's Google account (an access token), the household's list of approved people, and their Kindle and Google email addresses are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to, sold to, or shared with anyone else. Book files are deleted from that computer once sent, unless the owner chooses to keep a copy in their own library.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Seedflow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above, is never used for advertising, is never sold, and is not read by any person except where the owner needs to fix a problem with their own household's deliveries.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Google in Seedflow's settings at any time. Anyone can remove Seedflow's access to their Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Family members can ask the owner to remove their details, and they are deleted from the owner's computer.
