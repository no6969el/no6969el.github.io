Seedflow

A private, personal app for one household's media library.

Seedflow runs on a computer in the owner's home. It keeps the household's media library organised: it moves finished downloads into the right folders, and lets family members pick things to watch, play or read.

What it does with a Google account

The owner can connect their own Google account so that Seedflow can:

  • send ebooks from the owner's Gmail to a family member's Kindle email address, and
  • share audiobooks with family members through a folder in the owner's Google Drive.

Family members can also sign in to Seedflow's household web page with their Google account, so the owner can decide who has access.

Seedflow cannot read anyone's email, and only sees the Drive files it creates itself. See the privacy policy for details.

Who can use it

Seedflow is not a public service and has no sign-up. Only people the owner approves can use it.
